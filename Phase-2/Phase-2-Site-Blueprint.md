## 3. Google Sites Publishing & Security Settings

1. **Publish Settings:**
   - **Web address:** `master-company-portal` (or account default)
   - **Who can view my site:** Click **Manage** → Change "Published site" to **Restricted** (Only people added can open with the link).
   - **Search engines:** Uncheck "Request public search engines not to display my site" (irrelevant since site is restricted, but maintain privacy).
2. **Sharing Settings:**
   - **Owner / Editor:** Designated Admin Google Account only.
   - **General Access:** Restricted (Do NOT select "Anyone with the link").
   - **Viewers:** Authorized employee Google Accounts (View only).
